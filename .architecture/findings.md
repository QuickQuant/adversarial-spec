### FIND-001: Final Boss operational failure is returned as an approval-shaped verdict

- **Category:** error-handling
- **Severity:** error
- **Confidence:** high
- **Component:** gauntlet
- **Observation:** The optional final review documents `PASS` as permission to proceed, but its catch-all exception path returns `FinalBossVerdict.PASS` with a failure message. A downstream caller that keys on verdict cannot distinguish unavailable review from approval.
- **Suggestion:** Add an explicit failed/blocked Final Boss outcome and make promotion/exit behavior non-success for it.
- **Evidence:** `phase_7_final_boss.py:41-44,239-252`; verified with `rg -n -C 4 "Final Boss|final_boss|status.*PASS|PASS.*failure|except Exception"`.
